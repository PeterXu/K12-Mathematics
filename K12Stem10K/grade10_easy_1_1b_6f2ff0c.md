## 题目
已知全集 $U=\{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8\},A=\{3,4,5\},B=\{4,7,8\}$ .​​​​​​​‌（1）求 $\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}A,\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}B,\left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}A}\right )\cap \left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}B}\right ),\left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}A}\right )\cup \left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}B}\right )$ ；（2）验证 $\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}(A\cap B)=\left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}A}\right )\cup \left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}B}\right ),\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}(A\cup B)=\left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}A}\right )\cap \left ({\mathrm{∁}{{}_{U}}B}\right )$ .

## 解答

1. (1)∁ ${\rm \mathit{_{U}}\mathit{A}=\{1,2,6,7,8\}}$ ,∁_{U}B={1,2,3,5,6},(∁_{U}A)∩(∁_{U}B)={1,2,6},(∁_{U}A)∪(∁_{U}B)={1,2,3,5,6,7,8}.
2. (2)∵A∩B={4},∴∁ ${\rm \mathit{_{U}}(\mathit{A}∩\mathit{B})=\{1,2,3,5,6,7,8\}}$ .
3. ∵A∪B={3,4,5,7,8},∴∁ ${\rm \mathit{_{U}}(\mathit{A}∪\mathit{B})=\{1,2,6\}}$ .
4. 故有∁_{U}(A∩B)=(∁_{U}A)∪(∁_{U}B),∁_{U}(A∪B)=(∁_{U}A)∩(∁_{U}B).

**English**:
