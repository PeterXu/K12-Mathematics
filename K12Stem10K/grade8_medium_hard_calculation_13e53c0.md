## 题目
小明在探究二次根式时发现了下列两个有趣的变形：（一）一些分母含有二次根式加减的式子也可以分母有理化，如：$\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}+1}=\frac {\sqrt {2}-1}{\left ({\sqrt {2}+1}\right )\left ({\sqrt {2}-1}\right )}=\frac {\sqrt {2}-1}{2-1}=\sqrt {2}-1$ ；​​​​​​​‌$\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}=\frac {\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}}{\left ({\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}}\right )}=\frac {\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}}{3-2}=\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}$ ．（二）一些含根号的式子可以写成另一个式子的平方，如：${\left({\sqrt {3}+1}\right)^{2}}=4+2\sqrt {3}$ ， ${\left({\sqrt {5}+\sqrt {3}}\right)^{2}}=8+2\sqrt {15}$ ， ${\left({\sqrt {a}+\sqrt {b}}\right)^{2}}=a+b+2\sqrt {ab}\left ({a\geqslant 0,b\geqslant 0}\right )$ ；再根据平方根的定义可得 $\sqrt {4+2\sqrt {3}}=\sqrt {3}+1$ ， $\sqrt {8+2\sqrt {15}}=\sqrt {5}+\sqrt {3}$ ， $\sqrt {a+b+2\sqrt {ab}}=\sqrt {a}+\sqrt {b}\left ({a\geqslant 0,b\geqslant 0}\right )$ ；请回答下列问题：(1)归纳：观察上面的解题过程，请直接写出下列各式的结果．① $\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}=$； $\frac {1}{\sqrt {2n+1}+\sqrt {2n-1}}$ （n为正整数）=．②$\sqrt {7-4\sqrt {3}}=$；当 $1\leqslant x\leqslant 2$ 时，化简 $\sqrt {x-2\sqrt {x-1}}=$．(2)应用：求 $\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}+1}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {4}+\sqrt {3}}+\cdot \cdot \cdot +\frac {1}{\sqrt {2025}+\sqrt {2024}}$ 的值．(3)拓展：求 $\frac {1}{\sqrt {4-2\sqrt {3}}}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {8-2\sqrt {15}}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {12-2\sqrt {35}}}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {16-2\sqrt {63}}}$ 的值．

## 解答

1. （1）解：① $\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}=\frac {\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}}{\left ({\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}}\right )}=\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}$ ，
2. $\frac {1}{\sqrt {2n+1}+\sqrt {2n-1}}=\frac {\sqrt {2n+1}-\sqrt {2n-1}}{\left ({\sqrt {2n+1}+\sqrt {2n-1}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {2n+1}-\sqrt {2n-1}}\right )}=\frac {\sqrt {2n+1}-\sqrt {2n-1}}{2}$ ，
3. 故答案为 $\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}$ ； $\frac {\sqrt {2n+1}-\sqrt {2n-1}}{2}$ （n为正整数）；
4. ② $\sqrt {7-4\sqrt {3}}=\sqrt {{\left({\sqrt {3}}\right)^{2}}-4\sqrt {3}+2{^{2}}}=\sqrt {{\left({\sqrt {3}-2}\right)^{2}}}=2-\sqrt {3}$ ，
5. $\sqrt {x-2\sqrt {x-1}}=\sqrt {{\left({\sqrt {x-1}}\right)^{2}}-2\sqrt {x-1}+1{^{2}}}=1-\sqrt {x-1}$ ，
6. 故答案为 $2-\sqrt {3}$ ； $1-\sqrt {x-1}$ ；
7. （2） $\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}+1}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {4}+\sqrt {3}}+\cdot \cdot \cdot +\frac {1}{\sqrt {2025}+\sqrt {2024}}$
8. $=\sqrt {2}-1+\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}+\sqrt {4}-\sqrt {3}+\cdots \sqrt {2025}-\sqrt {2024}$
9. $=\sqrt {2025}-1$ ；
10. （3） $\frac {1}{\sqrt {4-2\sqrt {3}}}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {8-2\sqrt {15}}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {12-2\sqrt {35}}}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {16-2\sqrt {63}}}$
11. $=\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}-1}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {5}-\sqrt {3}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {5}}-\frac {1}{\sqrt {9}-\sqrt {7}}$
12. $=\frac {\sqrt {3}+1}{2}-\frac {\sqrt {5}+\sqrt {3}}{2}+\frac {\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {5}}{2}-\frac {\sqrt {9}+\sqrt {7}}{2}$
13. $=\frac {\sqrt {3}+1-\sqrt {5}-\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {5}-3-\sqrt {7}}{2}$
14. $=\frac {-2}{2}$ ，
15. $=-1$ ．
