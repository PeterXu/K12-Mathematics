## 题目
计算： $\frac {1}{-2}+\frac {1}{-6}+\frac {1}{-12}+\frac {1}{-20}+\frac {1}{-30}+\frac {1}{-42}=$．​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 原式 $=-\left ({\frac {1}{2}+\frac {1}{6}+\frac {1}{12}+\frac {1}{20}+\frac {1}{30}+\frac {1}{42}}\right )$ ，
2. $=-\left ({\frac {1}{1\times 2}+\frac {1}{2\times 3}+\frac {1}{3\times 4}+\frac {1}{4\times 5}+\frac {1}{5\times 6}+\frac {1}{6\times 7}}\right )$ ，
3. $=-\left ({1-\frac {1}{2}+\frac {1}{2}-\frac {1}{3}+\frac {1}{3}-\frac {1}{4}+\frac {1}{4}-\frac {1}{5}+\frac {1}{5}-\frac {1}{6}+\frac {1}{6}-\frac {1}{7}}\right )$ ，
4. $=-\left ({1-\frac {1}{7}}\right )$ ，
5. $=-\frac {6}{7}$ ．
