## 题目
先化简，再求值： $\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{3m{^{2}}-6m}+\left ({1-\frac {1}{m-2}}\right )$ ，其中 $m=\sqrt {3}$ ．​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. $\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{3m{^{2}}-6m}+\left ({1-\frac {1}{m-2}}\right )$
2. $=\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{3m(m-2)}+\left ({\frac {m-2}{m-2}-\frac {1}{m-2}}\right )$
3. $=\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{3m(m-2)}+\frac {m-3}{m-2}$
4. $=\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{3m(m-2)}+\frac {3m(m-3)}{3m(m-2)}$
5. $=\frac {4m{^{2}}-9m-9}{3m(m-2)}$
6. 当 $m=\sqrt {3}$ 时，原式 $=\frac {4\times 3-9\sqrt {3}-9}{3\sqrt {3}\times (\sqrt {3}-2)}=\frac {3\sqrt {3}-1}{2\sqrt {3}-3}=\frac {(3\sqrt {3}-1)(2\sqrt {3}+3)}{(2\sqrt {3}-3)(2\sqrt {3}+3)}=\frac {15+7\sqrt {3}}{3}$
