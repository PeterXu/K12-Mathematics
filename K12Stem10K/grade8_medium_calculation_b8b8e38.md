## 题目
阅读下列材料，然后解答下列问题：$\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}+1}=\frac {1\times \left ({\sqrt {2}-1}\right )}{\left ({\sqrt {2}-1}\right )\left ({\sqrt {2}+1}\right )}=\sqrt {2}-1$ ；​​​​​​​‌$\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}=\frac {1\times \left ({\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}}\right )}{\left ({\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}}\right )}=\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}$ ；以上这种化简的方法叫分母有理化．（1） $\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}$．（2） $\frac {1}{\sqrt {n}+\sqrt {n-1}}$ （ $n$ 为正整数）=．（3）化简： $\frac {1}{\sqrt {2}+1}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+\sqrt {2}}+\frac {1}{2+\sqrt {3}}+\cdots +\frac {1}{\sqrt {2025}+\sqrt {2024}}=$．（4）化简下列式子的值： $\frac {1}{\sqrt {3}+1}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {5}+\sqrt {3}}+\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {5}}+\cdots +\frac {1}{\sqrt {2025}+\sqrt {2023}}$

## 解答

1. （1）解： $\frac {1}{\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}=\frac {\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}}{\left ({\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {6}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}}\right )}=\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {6}$ .
2. （2）解： $\frac {1}{\sqrt {n}+\sqrt {n-1}}=\frac {\sqrt {n}-\sqrt {n-1}}{\left ({\sqrt {n}+\sqrt {n-1}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {n}-\sqrt {n-1}}\right )}=\sqrt {n}-\sqrt {n-1}$ .
3. （3）解：原式 $=\sqrt {2}-1+\sqrt {3}-\sqrt {2}+\sqrt {4}-\sqrt {3}+\cdots +\sqrt {2025}-\sqrt {2024}$
4. $=\sqrt {2025}-1$
5. $=44$ .
6. （4）解：原式 $=\frac {\sqrt {3}-1}{\left ({\sqrt {3}+1}\right )\left ({\sqrt {3}-1}\right )}+\frac {\sqrt {5}-\sqrt {3}}{\left ({\sqrt {5}+\sqrt {3}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {5}-\sqrt {3}}\right )}+\frac {\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {5}}{\left ({\sqrt {7}+\sqrt {5}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {5}}\right )}+\cdots +\frac {\sqrt {2025}-\sqrt {2023}}{\left ({\sqrt {2025}+\sqrt {2023}}\right )\left ({\sqrt {2025}-\sqrt {2023}}\right )}$
7. $=\frac {\sqrt {3}-1}{2}+\frac {\sqrt {5}-\sqrt {3}}{2}+\frac {\sqrt {7}-\sqrt {5}}{2}+\cdots +\frac {\sqrt {2025}-\sqrt {2023}}{2}$
8. $=\frac {\sqrt {2025}-1}{2}$
9. $=22$ ．
