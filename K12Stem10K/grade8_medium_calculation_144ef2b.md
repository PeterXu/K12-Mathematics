## 题目
先化简，再求值： $\left ({\frac {a{^{2}}-1}{a{^{2}}-2a+1}+\frac {1}{1-a}}\right )\div \frac {1}{a{^{2}}-a}$ ，其中 $a=2$ ．​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 解： $\left ({\frac {a{^{2}}-1}{a{^{2}}-2a+1}+\frac {1}{1-a}}\right )\div \frac {1}{a{^{2}}-a}$
2. $=\left [\frac {\left ({a+1}\right )\left ({a-1}\right )}{{\left({a-1}\right)^{2}}}-\frac {1}{a-1}\right ]\div \frac {1}{a\left ({a-1}\right )}$
3. $=\left ({\frac {a+1}{a-1}-\frac {1}{a-1}}\right )\div \frac {1}{a\left ({a-1}\right )}$
4. $=\frac {a}{a-1}\cdot a\left ({a-1}\right )$
5. $=a{^{2}}$ ，
6. 当 $a=2$ 时，原式 $=2{^{2}}=4$ ．
