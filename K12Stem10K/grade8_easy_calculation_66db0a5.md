## 题目
计算： $\frac {m{^{2}}}{m-3}+\frac {9}{3-m}$​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 解： $\frac {m{^{2}}}{m-3}+\frac {9}{3-m}=\frac {m{^{2}}}{m-3}-\frac {9}{m-3}=\frac {m{^{2}}-9}{m-3}=\frac {\left ({m-3}\right )\left ({m+3}\right )}{m-3}=m+3$ .

## 知识点

  - 分式的加减

**English**:
  - Addition and Subtraction of Rational Expressions
