## 题目
对于任意的x值都有 $\frac {2x+7}{x{^{2}}+x-2}=\frac {M}{x+2}+\frac {N}{x-1}$ ，则M，N值为（      ）​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 解： $\frac {M}{x+2}+\frac {N}{x-1}$
2. = $\frac {M\left ({x-1}\right )+N\left ({x+2}\right )}{\left ({x+2}\right )\left ({x-1}\right )}$
3. = $\frac {\hspace{-0.5em}\begin{array}{}\left ({M+N}\right )x+\left ({-M+2N}\right )\end{array}\hspace{-0.5em}}{x{^{2}}+x-2}$
4. ∴ $\frac {2x+7}{x{^{2}}+x-2}$ = $\frac {\hspace{-0.5em}\begin{array}{}\left ({M+N}\right )x+\left ({-M+2N}\right )\end{array}\hspace{-0.5em}}{x{^{2}}+x-2}$
5. ∴ $\begin{cases}M+N＝2\\-M+2N＝7\end{cases}$ ，
6. 解得： $\begin{cases}M＝-1\\N=3\end{cases}$ ，
7. 故选B．
