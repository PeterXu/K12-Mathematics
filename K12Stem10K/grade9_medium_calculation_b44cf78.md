# Mathematics - 题目 558

**ID**: b44cf78d00e547908727c8d90b3b5527
**年级**: Grade 9
**难度**: Medium
**题型**: Calculation

## 题目
先化简，再求值： $\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \left ({\frac {a{^{2}}-b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-2ab+b{^{2}}}+\frac {a}{b-a}}\right )$ ，其中a，b满足 $\sqrt {2a-b+2}+{\left({a+b-3}\right)^{2}}=0$ ，​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 解： $\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \left ({\frac {a{^{2}}-b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-2ab+b{^{2}}}+\frac {a}{b-a}}\right )$
2. $=\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \left ({\frac {\left ({a+b}\right )\left ({a-b}\right )}{{\left({a-b}\right)^{2}}}-\frac {a}{a-b}}\right )$
3. $=\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \left ({\frac {a+b}{a-b}-\frac {a}{a-b}}\right )$
4. $=\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \frac {a+b-a}{a-b}$
5. $=\frac {b{^{2}}}{a\left ({a-b}\right )}\div \frac {b}{a-b}$
6. $=\frac {b{^{2}}}{a\left ({a-b}\right )}\times \frac {a-b}{b}$
7. $=\frac {b}{a}$ ，
8. ∵ $\sqrt {2a-b+2}+{\left({a+b-3}\right)^{2}}=0$ ，
9. ∴ $\begin{cases}2a-b+2=0\\a+b-3=0\end{cases}$ ，
10. 解得 $\begin{cases}a=\frac {1}{3}\\b=\frac {8}{3}\end{cases}$ ，
11. ∴ $\frac {b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-ab}\div \left ({\frac {a{^{2}}-b{^{2}}}{a{^{2}}-2ab+b{^{2}}}+\frac {a}{b-a}}\right )$  $=\frac {b}{a}=\frac {8}{3}\div \frac {1}{3}=8$ ．
