## 题目
观察下列各式及其验证过程．$3\sqrt {\frac {3}{8}}=\sqrt {3+\frac {3}{8}}$ .​​​​​​​‌验证：$3\sqrt {\frac {3}{8}}=\sqrt {3{^{2}}}\times \sqrt {\frac {3}{8}}=\sqrt {\frac {3{^{3}}}{8}}=\sqrt {\frac {3{^{5}}-3+3}{3{^{2}}-1}}=$  $\sqrt {\frac {3(3{^{2}}-1)+3}{3{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {\frac {3(3{^{2}}-1)}{3{^{2}}-1}+\frac {3}{3{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {3+\frac {3}{8}}$同理可得： $4\sqrt {\frac {4}{15}}=\sqrt {4+\frac {4}{15}}$ ， $5\sqrt {\frac {5}{24}}=\sqrt {5+\frac {5}{24}}$ ，…通过上述探究你能猜测出： $a\sqrt {\frac {a}{a{^{2}}-1}}=$( $a>1$ )，并验证你的结论．

## 解答

1. $a\sqrt {\frac {a}{a{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {a{^{2}}}\times \sqrt {\frac {a}{a{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {\frac {a{^{3}}}{a{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {\frac {a{^{3}}-a+a}{a{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {\frac {a（a{^{2}}-1）+a}{a{^{2}}-1}}=\sqrt {a+\frac {a}{a{^{2}}-1}}$ ．
2. 故答案为 $\sqrt {a+\frac {a}{a{^{2}}-1}}$ ．
3. 【点睛】本题考查了二次根式的化简，读懂题目，熟练应用二次根式的性质进行化简是解题关键.
