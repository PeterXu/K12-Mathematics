## 题目
已知正方形 $ABCD$ 的对角线长为 $2$ ， $EF$ 是它的内切圆一条弦，点 $P$ 为正方形 $ABCD$ 四条边上的一个动点，当弦 $EF$ 的长度最大时， $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}$ 不可能为（      ）​​​​​​​‌

## 解答

1. 因为正方形 $ABCD$ 的对角线长为 $2$ ，所以边长为 $\sqrt {2}$ .
2. 建立如图示的平面直角坐标系，则正方形的内切圆的方程为 $x{{}^{2}}+y{{}^{2}}=\frac {1}{2}$
3. 
4. 当弦 $EF$ 的长度最大时， $EF$ 为圆的直径.设 $E\left ({m,n}\right )$ ，则 $F\left ({-m,-n}\right )$ ,且 $m{{}^{2}}+n{{}^{2}}=\frac {1}{2}$ .
5. 当点P在CD上时，可设 $P\left ({p,\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right ),\left ({-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ .
6. 则 $\overrightarrow {PE}=\left ({m-p,n-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right ),\overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({-m-p,-n-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({m-p,n-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )\cdot \left ({-m-p,-n-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )=p{{}^{2}}-m{{}^{2}}+\frac {1}{2}-n{{}^{2}}=p{{}^{2}}$ .
7. 因为 $-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}$ ，所以 $0\leqslant p{{}^{2}}\leqslant \frac {1}{2}$ .即 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}\in \left [0,\frac {1}{2}\right ]$ .
8. 当点P在AB上时，可设 $P\left ({p,-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right ),\left ({-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ .
9. 则 $\overrightarrow {PE}=\left ({m-p,n+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right ),\overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({-m-p,-n+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({m-p,n+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )\cdot \left ({-m-p,-n+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )=p{{}^{2}}-m{{}^{2}}+\frac {1}{2}-n{{}^{2}}=p{{}^{2}}$ .
10. 因为 $-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}$ ，所以 $0\leqslant p{{}^{2}}\leqslant \frac {1}{2}$ .即 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}\in \left [0,\frac {1}{2}\right ]$ .
11. 当点P在BC上时，可设 $P\left ({\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},p}\right ),\left ({-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ .
12. 则 $\overrightarrow {PE}=\left ({m-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},n-p}\right ),\overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({-m-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},-n-p}\right )$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({m-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},n-p}\right )\cdot \left ({-m-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},-n-p}\right )=\frac {1}{2}-m{{}^{2}}+p{{}^{2}}-n{{}^{2}}=p{{}^{2}}$ .
13. 因为 $-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}$ ，所以 $0\leqslant p{{}^{2}}\leqslant \frac {1}{2}$ .即 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}\in \left [0,\frac {1}{2}\right ]$ .
14. 当点P在AD上时，可设 $P\left ({-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},p}\right ),\left ({-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}\right )$ .
15. 则 $\overrightarrow {PE}=\left ({m+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},n-p}\right ),\overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({-m+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},-n-p}\right )$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}=\left ({m+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},n-p}\right )\cdot \left ({-m+\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},-n-p}\right )=\frac {1}{2}-m{{}^{2}}+p{{}^{2}}-n{{}^{2}}=p{{}^{2}}$ .
16. 因为 $-\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\leqslant p\leqslant \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}$ ，所以 $0\leqslant p{{}^{2}}\leqslant \frac {1}{2}$ .即 $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}\in \left [0,\frac {1}{2}\right ]$ .
17. 综上所述： $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}\in \left [0,\frac {1}{2}\right ]$ .
18. 对照四个选项, $\overrightarrow {PE}\cdot \overrightarrow {PF}$ 不可能为：AD.
19. 故选：AD.
