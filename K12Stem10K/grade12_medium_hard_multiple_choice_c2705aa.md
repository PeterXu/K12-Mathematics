## 题目

## 解答

1. $\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2{^{x}}}=\frac {2{^{\frac {1}{2}}}}{2{^{x}}}=\left ({\frac {1}{2}}\right ){^{x-\frac {1}{2}}}$ ，同理可得， $\frac {\sqrt[3]{3}}{3{^{y}}}=\left ({\frac {1}{3}}\right ){^{y-\frac {1}{3}}},\frac {\sqrt[7]{7}}{7{^{z}}}=\left ({\frac {1}{7}}\right ){^{z-\frac {1}{7}}}$ .
2. 令 $\left ({\frac {1}{2}}\right ){^{x-\frac {1}{2}}}=\left ({\frac {1}{3}}\right ){^{y-\frac {1}{3}}}=\left ({\frac {1}{7}}\right ){^{z-\frac {1}{7}}}=t\left ({t>0}\right )$ ，
3. 则
4. $\left ({x-\frac {1}{2}}\right ){\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}\frac {1}{2}=\left ({y-\frac {1}{3}}\right ){\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}\frac {1}{3}=\left ({z-\frac {1}{7}}\right ){\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}\frac {1}{7}={\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}t$ ，
5. 在同一直角坐标系中分别作出 $f\left ({x}\right )=\left ({x-\frac {1}{2}}\right ){\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}\frac {1}{2},g\left ({x}\right )=\left ({x-\frac {1}{3}}\right ){\rm{l}}{\rm{n}}\frac {1}{3}$ ，
